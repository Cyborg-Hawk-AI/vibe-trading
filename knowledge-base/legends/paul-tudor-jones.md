# Paul Tudor Jones (1954–)

**Known for:** Predicted and tripled his fund during the October 1987 crash
(reportedly +125% that month). Tudor Investment Corp compounded ~19% annually
for decades with rare down years. Macro/futures trader with legendary risk
discipline.

## Core method
1. **5:1 risk/reward.** "I'm looking for 5 to 1. Five-to-one means I can be
   wrong 80% of the time and still break even." Structure trades so the payoff
   asymmetry does the heavy lifting, not the hit rate.
2. **Defense first.** "The most important rule of trading is to play great
   defense, not great offense." He assumes every position is wrong until the
   market proves otherwise.
3. **200-day moving average as the master filter.** "My metric for everything
   out there is the 200-day moving average of closing prices... I want to be
   long above it, and out/short below it." He credits this single rule with
   keeping him out of every major disaster.
4. **Never average losers.** Decreases size when trading poorly, increases
   when trading well.
5. **Price makes news, not the other way.** Markets move first; narratives
   catch up.

## The 1987 trade
- His team mapped 1987's price structure onto 1929's chart (analog model) and
  concluded a crash was structurally possible; portfolio insurance (mechanical
  selling into declines) would accelerate it.
- Positioned short with defined risk into the setup; when the break came,
  Black Monday delivered the century's best single-day asymmetric payoff.

## Key quotes
- "Losers average losers."
- "Where you want to be is always in control, never wishing, always trading."
- "Don't focus on making money; focus on protecting what you have."
- "At the end of the day, the most important thing is how good are you at
  risk control."

## Lessons for a systematic trader
| PTJ principle | Systematic translation |
|---|---|
| 5:1 reward/risk | Wide targets vs tight stops; skew the payoff distribution |
| 200-day MA filter | Long-term trend gate before any entry signal |
| Defense first | Stops sized before entries; risk decided pre-trade |
| Losers average losers | Never add to a position in drawdown |
| Size by recent form | Reduce size after losses (anti-martingale) |

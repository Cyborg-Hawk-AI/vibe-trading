# Mark Minervini (1965–)

**Known for:** US Investing Championship winner (1997: +155%; 2021: +334%).
Claims a 5-year audited run averaging **220% per year** (33,500% compounded)
in the 1990s. Author of *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard*. The modern
refinement of O'Neil-style momentum breakout trading.

## SEPA (Specific Entry Point Analysis)
Five elements must align:
1. **Trend** — stock in a confirmed Stage 2 uptrend (see template below).
2. **Fundamentals** — accelerating earnings/sales/margins.
3. **Catalyst** — something driving institutional buying.
4. **Entry point** — low-risk pivot from a volatility contraction.
5. **Exit plan** — stop and target defined before entry.

## The Trend Template (all 8 must be true)
1. Price above both 150-day and 200-day MA.
2. 150-day MA above 200-day MA.
3. 200-day MA trending up ≥ 1 month.
4. 50-day MA above both 150 and 200.
5. Price above 50-day MA.
6. Price ≥ 30% above its 52-week low.
7. Price within 25% of its 52-week high.
8. Relative strength ranking ≥ 70 (prefer 90+).

This is a fully mechanical screen — directly implementable as entry gates.

## Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP)
His signature setup: successive pullbacks that **shrink** (e.g. 25% → 15% →
8% → 4%) with volume drying up at each contraction — supply exhausting.
Buy the breakout through the pivot on expanding volume. Tight stop just below
the pivot because a valid VCP breakout should not look back.

## Risk rules
- Average loss capped near **5–6%**, never past 10%.
- Risk/reward at entry minimum 2:1, target 3:1+.
- Progressive exposure: start small; only scale up after recent trades prove
  the environment ("trade larger only when winning").
- 50% batting average with 2:1 payoff = wealth; he often runs ~40–50%.

## Key quotes
- "The goal isn't to be right; it's to make big money when you're right and
  lose small when you're wrong."
- "Losing big is a choice."
- "Trade what you see, not what you think."

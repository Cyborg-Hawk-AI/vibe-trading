# Ray Dalio (1949–)

**Known for:** Founded Bridgewater Associates — the largest hedge fund in the
world (~$150B AUM at peak). Pure Alpha compounded ~12% annually for decades;
invented **risk parity** (All Weather, 1996). Author of *Principles*.

## Core ideas
1. **The economic machine.** Markets are driven by mechanical cause-effect
   relationships: productivity growth, the short-term debt cycle (5–8 years),
   and the long-term debt cycle (50–75 years). Understand where you are in
   the cycles and asset behavior becomes predictable-ish.
2. **Risk parity.** Traditional 60/40 is secretly ~90% equity risk. Instead,
   size each asset class so it contributes **equal risk**, levering low-vol
   assets (bonds) up. The portfolio then survives any of the four regimes:
   growth up/down × inflation up/down.
3. **The Holy Grail of investing.** "If you can find 15–20 good, uncorrelated
   return streams, you reduce risk ~80% without reducing return." This chart
   is arguably the most important slide in portfolio construction.
4. **Systemize your thinking.** Every rule Bridgewater believes is written
   down, coded, and backtested across all countries and centuries of data.
   Human judgment goes into the rules, not the trades.
5. **Radical transparency / idea meritocracy.** Stress-test every belief;
   the biggest risk is being wrong and not knowing it.

## Key quotes
- "He who lives by the crystal ball will eat shattered glass."
- "Pain + Reflection = Progress."
- "The biggest mistake investors make is to believe that what happened in the
  recent past is likely to persist."

## Practical machinery worth stealing
- **Vol-weighted allocation:** weight positions inversely to their volatility
  so each contributes similar risk (the core of risk parity at any scale).
- **Regime quadrant:** classify each period by growth and inflation surprise
  direction; know which sleeve profits in which quadrant.
- **Correlation as the free lunch:** adding a *worse* but uncorrelated sleeve
  can improve the portfolio more than improving the best sleeve.

## Lessons for a systematic trader
| Dalio principle | Systematic translation |
|---|---|
| 15 uncorrelated streams | Run multiple sleeves (equity/crypto/penny) and weight by correlation |
| Risk parity | Inverse-vol position weighting inside each sleeve |
| Timeless & universal rules | Test rules across symbols/periods before trusting them |
| Pain + reflection | Automated post-loss diagnostics feeding the next iteration |
| Regime quadrants | Market-state classifiers gating which sleeve is active |

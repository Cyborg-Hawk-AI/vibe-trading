# William O'Neil (1933–2023)

**Known for:** CAN SLIM — the most widely used growth-momentum stock-selection
framework. Founded Investor's Business Daily. Turned $5,000 into $200,000 in
26 months (1962–63); bought a NYSE seat at 30.

## CAN SLIM framework
Each letter is a screenable, testable condition:

| Letter | Meaning | Testable proxy |
|---|---|---|
| **C** | Current quarterly EPS growth ≥ 25% | Earnings surprise/growth data |
| **A** | Annual EPS growth ≥ 25% for 3 years | Fundamental history |
| **N** | New product/management/price high | 52-week-high proximity |
| **S** | Supply & demand — small float + volume surge on up moves | Volume vs. median on advances |
| **L** | Leader, not laggard — RS rating ≥ 80 | Relative strength vs. universe (top quintile momentum) |
| **I** | Institutional sponsorship increasing | Fund-ownership data |
| **M** | Market direction — trade with the general market | Index above key MAs; distribution-day count |

## Chart patterns he codified
- **Cup with handle** — a rounded base, a shallow pullback (handle), buy the
  handle-high breakout on volume ≥ 40–50% above average.
- **Flat base**, **double bottom**, **high tight flag** — all breakout
  structures with volume confirmation.

## Sell rules (as important as the buys)
- **Cut every loss at 7–8%** below purchase price. No exceptions, no thinking.
- Sell into climactic strength (25%+ gains in 1–3 weeks after a long run).
- Sell on break of the 50-day line on heavy volume.

## The math he preached
- You only need to be right ~1 in 3 if winners run 25%+ and losers stop at 7%.
- Most true market leaders gained 100–1000% — the whole game is holding the
  handful of monsters and clipping everything else quickly.

## The "M" is the big one
O'Neil's studies: **3 of 4 stocks follow the general market's direction.**
No stock selection survives a bear market; the distribution-day count (4–5
institutional selling days in a few weeks) was his regime-off switch.

## Lessons for a systematic trader
| O'Neil principle | Systematic translation |
|---|---|
| RS ≥ 80 | Cross-sectional momentum ranking (top quintile only) |
| Volume-confirmed breakout | Breakout gate + volume-vs-median filter |
| 7–8% hard stop | Fixed max-loss stop independent of ATR |
| Market direction filter | Index-level regime gate before any entry |
| 52-week-high proximity | "New high" as a feature, not a fear |

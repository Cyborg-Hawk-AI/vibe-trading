# Peter Lynch (1944–)

**Known for:** Ran Fidelity Magellan 1977–1990: **29.2% annualized**, beating
the S&P 500 in 11 of 13 years, growing AUM from $18M to $14B. Author of
*One Up on Wall Street* and *Beating the Street*. Coined "tenbagger."

## Core method: Growth At a Reasonable Price (GARP)
1. **Buy what you understand.** Edge comes from knowing the company's actual
   business — he found winners in malls and pantries before Wall Street did
   (Hanes, Taco Bell, Dunkin').
2. **Earnings drive prices — eventually.** "People may bet on hourly wiggles,
   but it's the earnings that waggle the wiggle, long term."
3. **PEG ratio.** P/E divided by growth rate. PEG < 1 = attractively priced
   growth. His preferred single valuation shortcut.
4. **Category the stock first.** Six types with different playbooks:
   slow growers, stalwarts, fast growers (the tenbagger hunting ground),
   cyclicals, turnarounds, asset plays.
5. **The story.** Every holding must have a two-minute thesis you can state
   plainly — and you sell when the story changes, not when the price does.

## Notable views
- "Selling your winners and holding your losers is like cutting the flowers
  and watering the weeds."
- "Far more money has been lost by investors preparing for corrections than
  has been lost in corrections themselves."
- "Know what you own, and know why you own it."
- Small investors have an edge over institutions: they can buy small/ignored
  names and act instantly without committees.

## What made the record (his own postmortem)
- He owned up to 1,400 stocks, but returns were driven by a small number of
  tenbaggers held for years — power-law payoff distribution.
- Turnover was high in small losers, near-zero in confirmed winners.
- The big money was made in year 3, 4, 5 of a winner — patience with
  confirmation, not prediction.

## Lessons for a systematic trader
| Lynch principle | Systematic translation |
|---|---|
| Earnings waggle the wiggle | Fundamental growth filters on the universe |
| PEG < 1 | Valuation-adjusted momentum screening |
| Water the flowers | Let winners run; recycle capital out of laggards fast |
| Power-law payoffs | Position for fat right tails; don't cap winners early |
| Story changed = sell | Exit on thesis (signal) invalidation, not price noise |

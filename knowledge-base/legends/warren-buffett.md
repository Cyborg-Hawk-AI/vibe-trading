# Warren Buffett (1930–)

**Known for:** ~**19.9% annualized at Berkshire Hathaway 1965–2023** vs ~10%
for the S&P — nearly 60 years, turning compounding into the greatest fortune
in investing history. Student of Benjamin Graham; evolved value investing from
"cigar butts" to "wonderful companies at fair prices."

## Core method
1. **Circle of competence.** Only act where you genuinely understand the
   economics. The size of the circle matters less than knowing its boundary.
2. **Economic moats.** Durable competitive advantages — brands, network
   effects, switching costs, cost advantages — protect returns on capital
   from competition. The moat, not the growth rate, is the asset.
3. **Margin of safety** (from Graham). Buy at enough of a discount to
   intrinsic value that being somewhat wrong still works out.
4. **Concentration.** "Diversification is protection against ignorance. It
   makes little sense if you know what you are doing." Berkshire's returns
   came from a handful of enormous positions (Coke, AmEx, Apple).
5. **Time arbitrage.** His edge is the willingness to hold for decades while
   the market obsesses over quarters. "Our favorite holding period is forever."
6. **Temperament over intellect.** "Be fearful when others are greedy and
   greedy when others are fearful."

## The float engine
Underrated mechanics: insurance float gave Berkshire ~1.6:1 costless
structural leverage on a conservative book — a large share of the
outperformance (formalized in the AQR paper *Buffett's Alpha*: leverage on
cheap, safe, quality stocks explains the record).

## Rules and quotes
- "Rule No. 1: Never lose money. Rule No. 2: Never forget rule No. 1."
  (About avoiding permanent capital impairment, not avoiding volatility.)
- "The stock market is a device for transferring money from the impatient to
  the patient."
- "Price is what you pay. Value is what you get."
- "It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair
  company at a wonderful price." (Munger's influence)

## Relevance to short-horizon trading
Buffett's method is mostly the *opposite* of active trading — that's the
point of including him. What transfers:
| Buffett principle | Systematic translation |
|---|---|
| Never lose money | Capital preservation gates rank above return gates |
| Quality factor | Quality/profitability screens improve any equity universe |
| Fear/greed cycles | Sentiment extremes as contrarian regime signals |
| Cheap leverage | Structural cost of capital matters (fees, funding, margin) |
| Patience premium | Longer holding windows harvest edges fast traders pay away |

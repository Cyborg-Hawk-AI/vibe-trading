# Larry Williams (1942–)

**Known for:** Turned **$10,000 into $1,147,000 (+11,376%) in 12 months** to
win the 1987 World Cup Championship of Futures Trading — still the record.
Inventor of Williams %R, the COT-based sentiment approach, and dozens of
short-term futures patterns. Author of *Long-Term Secrets to Short-Term Trading*.

## Core method
1. **Short-term swings inside conditional setups.** He doesn't trade patterns
   in isolation — a pattern only fires when *conditions* (trend, day of week,
   COT positioning, seasonality) make it favorable. Setup = condition + trigger.
2. **Volatility breakouts.** Signature entry: buy tomorrow at
   `open + k × (today's range)` — a move beyond a volatility threshold proves
   real buying. This simple structure has been the backbone of many CTA systems.
3. **Commercials' positioning (COT report).** Trade in the direction the
   commercial hedgers (the "smart money") are accumulating, against public
   sentiment extremes.
4. **Bail-out exit.** Exit on the **first profitable open** after entry — take
   the market's first gift on short-horizon trades rather than round-tripping.
5. **Seasonality & calendar effects.** Trading day of month, holidays,
   day-of-week biases — small persistent edges he stacked onto entries.

## The 1987 contest run — the honest lesson
He risked colossal fractions of the account (Kelly-style aggression); the
account went from $10K to $2.2M, **drew down to ~$700K**, then finished at
$1.1M. He has said openly the sizing was reckless and survivable only in a
contest. His daughter Michelle later won the same contest (+1,000%) with
tamer risk. Takeaway: the *entries* had real edge; the sizing turned a great
year into a rollercoaster that would kill most accounts.

## Key quotes
- "Wealth is created by the ability to hold on to winning positions... the
  longer the time frame, the more money you make."
- "Trading is the business of making mistakes and managing them."
- "The market rewards the trader who does the analysis before the trade."

## Lessons for a systematic trader
| Williams principle | Systematic translation |
|---|---|
| Volatility breakout | Entry at open + k×ATR threshold |
| Conditions + trigger | Composite gates: regime AND pattern, never pattern alone |
| First profitable open | Time-based/first-profit exits for short-horizon trades |
| COT smart money | Positioning/sentiment data as a directional filter |
| His drawdown | Edge discovery and position sizing are separate problems |

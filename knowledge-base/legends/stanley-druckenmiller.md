# Stanley Druckenmiller (1953–)

**Known for:** ~**30% annualized over 30 years with zero losing years** at
Duquesne Capital. Lead portfolio manager of Soros's Quantum Fund during the
sterling trade. Widely considered the greatest money-maker alive.

## Core method
1. **Concentration, not diversification.** "The only way to make superior
   returns is to concentrate heavily... put all your eggs in one basket and
   watch the basket very carefully." He routinely put 30%+ into a single theme
   when everything lined up.
2. **Never invest in the present.** Markets price the future; he positions for
   the world **18 months out**, especially liquidity conditions.
3. **Liquidity drives markets.** Earnings move individual stocks; central-bank
   liquidity moves the whole market. "Focus on the central banks and the
   movement of liquidity."
4. **It's a bet-sizing game.** Being right 50% of the time is fine if wins are
   sized 5–10× larger than losses. Home runs when right, bunts when unsure.
5. **When you're hot, press; when cold, shrink.** He scales exposure with his
   own recent performance — the systematic analogue is equity-curve-based
   exposure control.

## Famous moments
- 1992 sterling short (with Soros): his idea, Soros's sizing lesson.
- Went from -12% to +40%+ in 1999 by reversing his own tech short and riding
  the bubble — then gave much back in 2000 fighting it. His stated lesson:
  emotional capitulation trades are the most dangerous.

## Key quotes
- "Soros taught me it's not whether you're right or wrong, it's how much you
  make when you're right and how much you lose when you're wrong."
- "The first thing I heard when I got in the business... let your profits run
  and cut your losses. It's still the most important."
- "I've learned many things from George, but perhaps the most significant is
  that it takes courage to be a pig." (on sizing winners)

## Lessons for a systematic trader
| Druckenmiller principle | Systematic translation |
|---|---|
| Concentrate when aligned | Increase weight when multiple signals agree |
| 18 months out | Longer-horizon regime filters over short-term noise |
| Liquidity focus | Market-regime gates (rate trends, index breadth) |
| Press when hot | Equity-curve based position scaling |
| Asymmetric sizing | Larger size on higher-conviction (multi-gate) setups |
